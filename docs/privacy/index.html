This document has two parts: the privacy policy, which explains what data Musical Showdown uses, and the terms of use, which set the rules for playing it. By installing or using the game you accept both. The Spanish version, Español, is the original; if the two differ, the Spanish one prevails.

Privacy, in short
  • There are no user accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • What you type in the game (player names, settings) stays on your device.
  • The microphone, if you use it, only measures volume: it does not record or send audio.
  • The camera only reads the QR code used to link devices.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for commercial purposes.
Terms, in short
  • The game is for having fun at parties and get-togethers; use it respectfully.
  • You can play for free; some features are paid (Premium).
  • The game does not play or download third-party music.
  • It is provided "as is," without warranties, and may change over time.

Part I

Privacy policy

What information the game uses, what for, and who it is shared with.

1Who is responsible

This policy explains what information Musical Showdown (the "game") uses, what it uses it for, who it is shared with, and what you can do about it. It applies to the game on Android, Windows, Linux and the web, including the demo version.

Musical Showdown is an independently developed game. The controller of the information described here is its developer, based in Colombia. For any privacy question, write to showdownmusical@gmail.com.

2What information we handle

2.1 What is stored on your device

The game stores the following on your own device, and on no server of ours:

  • Your settings: sound, animations and countdown.
  • The names of players and teams and the options of your last match, so you don't have to type them again.
  • The notices you have already read.
  • Your purchase status (if you have Premium), so the game works without internet.
  • If you linked a phone to a PC, the address of that PC on your network and a key to reconnect.

All of this is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

2.2 What we do not collect

We do not ask for or store your real name, email, phone number, address, location, contacts, photos or voice recordings. There are no profiles, messages or content that you publish for other people. Please do not type sensitive data (health, religion, political views, etc.) in player names.

2.3 Information received by third parties

Some services the game uses (for example Google for notifications, GitHub for notices, and the store where you installed it) may receive technical data such as the IP address or the device identifier, according to their own policies. See section 6.

3Permissions and what they are for

PermissionWhat forWhat happens to the information

4Linking a phone to a PC

When you use the phone as a controller for the PC, the two devices talk directly inside your Wi-Fi network, without going through the internet or any server of ours. Only what is needed for the match travels between them (what is on screen and what is tapped). If you move a match from the phone to the PC, that match's data (names and scores) is sent to your PC.

5What we use the information for

  • Making the game work: remembering your settings and players, linking devices and checking your purchases. Legal basis: performance of the service you requested.
  • Telling you about news: new versions and important notices. Legal basis: legitimate interest; for system notifications, your consent when you grant the permission.
  • Security and maintenance: keeping the game stable and protecting connections. Legal basis: legitimate interest and legal compliance.
  • Complying with the law and defending our rights if necessary.

We do not build profiles, we do not make automated decisions that affect you, and we do not use your information for targeted advertising.

6Third-party services and who we share with

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google), Android only: sends notifications. Google assigns your device a notification identifier, which we do not link to your identity. You can turn notifications off in Android settings. Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub: the game reads a public page to show notices and new versions, like any web page. No personal data is sent. GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • YouTube: the button to listen to a song opens a search in your browser. The game does not play or download music; what you do on YouTube is governed by the policies of YouTube and Google.
  • Google AdMob, once ads are active (free version on Android only): shows non-personalized ads. See section 8.
  • Stores (Microsoft Store, Google Play): if you installed the game from a store, it may collect data under its own policy.

We may also disclose information to authorities when the law requires it, and to professional advisers or to whoever acquires the game if it is transferred. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Usage analytics: the game uses no statistics or crash-reporting tools, and has no server of its own where information about you is stored.

7In-game purchases

Some features (such as the Alternativo 2 mode and listening to the singing) are unlocked with a purchase or subscription. Payment is processed by the store (Google Play or Microsoft Store): we do not receive or store your payment details. The game only asks the store whether the purchase is active.

8Ads

Today the game shows no ads. The free version for phones (Android) will show them later; Premium removes them, and the demo and the PC versions do not have them. Once they are active, they will work like this:

8.1 Who shows them and where

Ads will be served by Google AdMob. They will appear on the home, setup, result and podium screens, and never during a round.

8.2 Non-personalized ads

We will show non-personalized ads: they are chosen by context (for example the type of app, the language and the approximate country) and not from a profile of your interests. We do not use your information for targeted advertising.

8.3 What data AdMob may receive

To show the ad and measure that it was seen, Google may receive technical data from your device, such as the IP address, the device or advertising identifier, the model and the system, and how you interact with the ad. We do not send it your player names, your settings or your scores. More information: how Google uses advertising data.

8.4 Your choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Union and the United Kingdom), we will ask for your consent before showing ads.
  • You can reset or delete the advertising identifier in Android settings.
  • You can remove ads with Premium.

If we change the type of ads or the provider, we will update this policy before doing so.

9How long information is kept

The information the game stores lives on your device and you keep it until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Third-party services (section 6) keep their own data under their own policies. We do not keep user databases.

10International transfers

Some services the game uses (Google, GitHub, the stores) have servers in other countries, such as the United States, where data protection may differ from that of your country. Those companies apply their own safeguards (for example, standard contractual clauses). We do not send personal information to servers of our own.

11Teens and minors

Musical Showdown is intended for teens and adults. It is not meant for children under 13 (or the minimum age set by the law of your country) and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe information about a child was sent to us, write to us and we will delete it.

12Your rights

12.1 In general

Because we do not store personal data on servers of our own, your information is on your device and you control it: you can delete it (Android: Settings → Apps → Musical Showdown → Storage) or uninstall the game.

12.2 If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom

You have the right to request access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction and objection to the processing of your information, and to withdraw your consent where we have relied on it. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country.

12.3 If you are in the United States (California and other states)

You may ask to know what information we hold, to have it corrected or deleted, and to opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising. We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising, and we do not discriminate against anyone who exercises these rights.

12.4 How to exercise them

Write to showdownmusical@gmail.com saying what you want. We may ask for details to confirm who you are. If we cannot handle your request, we will explain why.

13Your options

  • Notifications: turn them off in the system settings.
  • Permissions: you can remove them at any time in the system settings.
  • Do Not Track: the game does not track your activity, so browser "Do Not Track" signals change nothing.
  • Cancel Premium: from your Google Play or Microsoft Store account.

14Security

Connections to services on the internet use encryption (https). Linking between devices only works inside your network and with a code that changes every minute. Even so, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

15Links to other sites

The game may open links to third-party sites (for example YouTube or the stores). We do not control them and are not responsible for their practices; we recommend reading their policies.

16Changes to this policy

We may change this policy. If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. Important changes will also be announced in the game's notice bell.
